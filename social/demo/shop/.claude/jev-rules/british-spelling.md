---
description: Writing or editing prose people will read, such as documentation, README files, UI copy, error messages, comments or commit messages.
---
Use British spelling in everything people read: organise, colour, behaviour, licence as a noun, license as a verb.

- No em-dashes. Use a comma, a full stop or brackets instead.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Code identifiers keep their existing spelling (a function called `colorize` stays `colorize`).
