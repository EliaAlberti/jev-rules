description: Writing a commit message, creating a commit, or opening a pull request.
---
Commits follow Conventional Commits.

- `type(scope): summary` in the imperative, under 72 characters. Types: feat, fix, docs, refactor, test, chore.
- One logical change per commit. The body says why, not what.
- A breaking change gets `!` and a BREAKING CHANGE footer.
